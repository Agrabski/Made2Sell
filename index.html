A week at a fair

What a fair actually looks like

Every market runs the same loop. Made2Sell follows it from the first prep list to the final profit.

Two weeks before

Plan the stall

You add the fair and build a prep list - 20 candles, 15 soaps, 10 of the new bath bombs.

Works out the materials you'll need, and tells you what you're short of before you order.

The week before

Produce the stock

You work down the list batch by batch - a tray of soap on Monday, candles across Wednesday evening.

The production timer records how long a batch really takes, and materials come off your stock as you make. That's where your true labour cost comes from.

Friday night

Pack the crate

You tick things off against your targets as they go into the box.

Planned vs. packed, product by product - so you drive off knowing exactly what's in the van.

Saturday, 9am

Sell all day

Tap, tap, done. Someone pays, you're already serving the next person.

Sale mode updates stock and takings on every sale - in a field, in a hall, with no signal at all.

Saturday, 6pm

See how it really went

You're still folding up the table.

Revenue, cost of goods, the stall fee and your net profit - before the crate is back in the car.

Questions

Frequently asked

When can I use Made2Sell?

Made2Sell is in closed alpha - the screens above are the real, working app. We're refining it with a small group of testers and will open up broader testing as it matures. Email us to be notified.

Which platforms are supported?

Android phones and tablets today. An iOS version is planned for later.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Everything works offline - perfect for a busy market stall - and your data stays on your device.

What does it cost?

It's free, with no ads. Donations will be welcome in future to help keep it going, and there may one day be optional paid integrations with online retailers - but the core app stays free.

Is my data private?

Yes. There's no account and no third-party servers - your data lives on your phone. Back it up and restore it from a file, or opt in to Google Drive backup, which uploads only to your own Drive's private app folder. Nothing is ever sent to us.

Can I back up to the cloud?

Yes, optionally. Alongside file backups, you can turn on Google Drive backup to automatically save a copy to your own Google Drive on a schedule you set - and restore it on any device you sign in on. It uses your Drive's private app folder, so only you can see it.

What if I find bugs?

Bugs are expected in an alpha - that's what testing is for. Testers report issues directly and help shape what gets fixed next.
